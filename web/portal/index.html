Your projects stay with you.

Open Projects & board in the editor to save named projects, restore your workspace or import and export .ubp files. Projects are stored in this browser on this site, not sent to a server.

Clearing site data, private browsing or browser storage eviction can remove local saves. Export files for backups and to move between devices. Wi-Fi passwords are never included in browser project storage.
